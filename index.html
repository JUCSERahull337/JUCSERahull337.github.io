01 / About

About

My research centers on Trustworthy AI and AI Security — understanding how machine learning systems fail, can be attacked, or can be made more robust, transparent, and safe to deploy. Driven by the challenge of "black-box" algorithms, this extends naturally into Explainable AI and Federated Learning, where I work on privacy-preserving, decentralized approaches that let collaborators train models securely without sharing raw data. Much of my applied work has been in Healthcare AI, using these techniques to build interpretable, privacy-respecting systems for real clinical settings.
